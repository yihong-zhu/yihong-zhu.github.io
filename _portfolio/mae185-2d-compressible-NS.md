For UCSD's MAE185: Computational Fluid Mechanics (Winter 2024), I wrote a 2D finite difference solver for the compressible Navier-Stokes equations and applied it to the same rectangular domain with two setups: shock induced by leading edge and shock-boundary-layer-interaction.

Both problems have inlet Mach numbers of 4.0
